# Project Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Business owners, SME operators, startup founders, corporate managers, and local businesses in Samut Sakhon (Mahachai, Krathum Baen, Ban Phaeo) and Bangkok.
- **Use Cases**: They need a professional, fast, SEO-ready, and conversion-optimized website to represent their business, gain customers, and rank on Google.
- **User Goals**: Trusting a local tech agency, learning about service packages, reviewing portfolios, and getting in touch easily (via LINE or by requesting a custom quote).

### Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone**: Professional, technical, trustworthy, and cutting-edge but highly approachable for local SMEs.
- **Emotional Goals**:Evoking confidence, security, modern technical excellence, and clarity.
- **Key Words**: Fast, SEO-ready, Premium.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Style**: Premium Dark Tech Agency.
- **Color Palette**: Deep dark slate base (`#020617` / `#0f172a`), neon blue primary glow accents (`#38bdf8`), subtle green LINE CTAs (`#06c755`), and warning/performance amber highlights (`#fbbf24`).
- **Layout & Rhythm**: Fluid container margins, glassmorphism panel containers (used with restraint and purpose), and smooth transition effects.
- **Aesthetic Anti-references**: Over-glowing AI gradients, generic stock vectors, layout elements with hard borders, and centered card repetition.

### Design Principles
1. **Performance and Core Web Vitals First**: Sub-second load times, no render-blocking video blocks above the fold, and optimized Next-gen layout images.
2. **Clear Conversion Hierarchy**: Primary action is LINE messaging (easy, quick for Thai users), secondary action is requesting custom quotations (QuoteModal).
3. **Local SEO Integration**: Clear, readable local service keywords integrated into natural Thai copy.
4. **Visual rhythm over card wrapping**: Avoid monotonous repeating grids of identical cards. Build asymmetric layouts, stacked lists, and fluid spacing.
